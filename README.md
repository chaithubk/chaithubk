## 🧠 About Me

I am a **Software Architect II at Philips Healthcare** with 18+ years of specialization in
safety-critical patient monitoring systems: bedside platforms that track vital signs, prioritize
alarms, and support clinical decision-making in ICUs and cardiac care.

In my Software Architect role, I have repeatedly taken on leadership responsibilities
to help build new teams, align cross-functional stakeholders, and move initiatives from concept
to execution across standalone patient-monitoring and edge medical-device platforms.

My experience spans the full technology stack:

- ⚙️ **Hardware & OS** — Yocto BSP, embedded Linux hardening, bare-metal firmware
- 💻 **Languages & Protocols** — C++17, MQTT, AMQP, HL7
- 🎨 **Clinical UI** — Qt6/QML, IEC 60601-1-8 compliant designs
- 🛡️ **Regulatory** — IEC 62304, SBOM, FDA cybersecurity guidance, DHF traceability

I am now focused on scaling from **"how to build"** to **"what to build, why, and when"**,
with increasing ownership across product strategy, prioritization, and cross-functional delivery.
Through an independent portfolio, I validate MedTech architectural and regulatory decisions in
real implementation environments.
